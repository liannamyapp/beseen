03

What we do

Leave the marketing to us.

We run the month: film, feed, ads, and one report. You run the room.

  1. 01

    Social media, kept.

    Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube. 12–20 professional posts a month, written and scheduled. A shared calendar you approve before anything goes live. Captions, tags, and language for the city. A monthly read of reach, saves, and what actually booked a table.

    Instagram · YouTube · Facebook · TikTok

  2. 02

    Ads that know who is hungry.

    Google, Seznam, TripAdvisor, Yelp. We set the accounts, split tourists from locals, and build a funnel that respects the budget. Weekly optimisation. We track cost per reservation — not vanity reach. No spray. No pray.

    Google · Seznam · TripAdvisor · Yelp

  3. 03

    Pictures and films of the room.

    On-site photo and film. Lighting, food styling, chef hands, the service floor after nine. Reels cut for TikTok and Instagram. A library you can use on the site, in ads, and in print. Shot for the feed, not for a brochure.

    TikTok · Instagram · YouTube

  4. 04

    One number. Three that explain it.

    Eight KPIs, every month: total reach, new followers, engagement, Reels views, messages and bookings, average CPC, the winning post, new reviews. Then what we change next. One month gives context. Three months tell the truth.

    Sent at the start of the month. Always.

  5. 05

    A site that does not apologise.

    The digital front door. Fast on a phone, menu with prices and pictures, a reservation widget, Czech / English / Armenian if the room needs it. Lookers become bookers.

  6. 06

    The room next door.

    Competitor posting, the content that worked, tone, review velocity, search rank. Hospitality and social trends. Target behaviour. Then a written plan for the next thirty days.

  1. 01

    Film the room

    On-site stills and reels. Plates, hands, the hour that sells the night.

  2. 02

    Keep the feed

    12–20 posts. Calendar you sign off. Copy in the languages the city uses.

  3. 03

    Spend where hunger is

    Google, Seznam, maps, reviews. Tourists and locals as two audiences. Weekly spend check.

  4. 04

    Send the number

    Eight KPIs on day one. Then the three changes for the month ahead.
